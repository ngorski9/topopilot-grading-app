Since this TTK build has no ready-made "asymmetric tensor topology" filter, I implemented the standard algorithm directly (`/workspace/eigenvector_partition.py`):
- Decomposed each tensor into `E=(A-D)/2`, `F=(B+C)/2`, `W=(C-B)/2`.
- Eigenvector partition: real domain where `E²+F²-W² ≥ 0`, complex domain otherwise — rasterized at 10 px/unit-square via bilinear interpolation.
- Degenerate points: exact zeros of `(E,F)` on the piecewise-linear triangulated field (2 triangles/cell, solved analytically per triangle), classified wedge/trisector by the sign of the local Jacobian of `(E,F)`, drawn as radius-1 circles (white=wedge, pink=trisector).
