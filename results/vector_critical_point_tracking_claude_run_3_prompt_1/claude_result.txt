Done. Summary of the pipeline in `/workspace/track_critical_points.py`:

1. **Data loading** — reads `cylinder1/2/3.vti` (first 3 time steps) with VTK, extracting the single-component `u`/`v` arrays on the 150×450 image grid.
2. **Critical-point extraction** — since TTK 1.3 has no dedicated vector-field critical point filter, each pixel quad is split into 2 triangles and the PL zero of the linearly-interpolated `(u,v)` field is solved via barycentric coordinates; classified as source/sink (red) vs. saddle (blue) by the sign of the Poincaré index (ratio of vector-space to geometric orientation).
3. **Tracking via partial optimal transport** — for each pair of consecutive time steps, `ot.partial.partial_wasserstein` (POT library) computes a transport plan between the critical-point sets using Euclidean cost, with the transported mass capped below 1 so births/deaths of critical points are handled naturally as unmatched mass rather than forced matches.
4. **Rendering** — the 3 time steps are drawn as glyphed arrow fields (colored by velocity magnitude) offset along Z, with tracked critical points as spheres and green tubes linking their trajectories across time.

Outputs written to `/workspace/`:
- `cylinder_tracked_critical_points.png` — rendered visualization (vortex shedding field + tracked critical points)
- `cylinder_critical_points.vtp`, `cylinder_tracks.vtp` — the critical points and track polylines for further inspection in ParaView

Console output also lists all 89 tracks found (most persist source→saddle pairs across all 3 steps; a few appear/disappear, correctly left unmatched by the partial transport).
