This clearly shows distinct Morse-Smale segments (each a basin between a minimum, saddle, and maximum), with red spheres at maxima, white at saddles, and blue at minima, all radius 2. The pipeline used:

1. `XMLImageDataReader` to load `fracture.vti` (scalar field `Scalars_`)
2. `TTKPersistenceDiagram` → `Threshold` (persistence ≥ 0.05) → `TTKTopologicalSimplification` to simplify the field
3. `TTKMorseSmaleComplex` on the simplified field with critical points, separatrices, and segmentation enabled
4. Critical points split by `CellDimension` (0=min, 1=saddle, 2=max) into colored sphere glyphs (radius 2, blue/white/red)
5. Segmentation surface (`MorseSmaleManifold`) rendered underneath, colored categorically
