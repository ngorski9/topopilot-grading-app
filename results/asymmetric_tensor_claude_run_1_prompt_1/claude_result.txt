**Approach used** (TTK ships no ready-made "asymmetric tensor topology" filter, so this was computed directly from the field, using the standard Delmarcelle & Hesselink 1994 construction that the terms "trisector"/"wedge" come from):

- Degenerate points: zeros of the symmetric-deviator vector field `E=(A−D)/2, F=(B+C)/2`, found per-cell via exact bilinear-zero solving. Classified by the local Jacobian sign of `(E,F)`: negative → **trisector**, positive → **wedge**.
