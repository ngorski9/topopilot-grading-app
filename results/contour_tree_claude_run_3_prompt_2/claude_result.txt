The visualization now shows the raw scalar field as a viridis volume rendering alongside the simplified piecewise-linear contour tree overlay (gray tubes for arcs, spheres colored red=maxima, orange=2-saddles, white=1-saddles, blue=minima), all successfully generated with TTK/ParaView.

Summary of what was done:
- Loaded the 21-timestep Ionization time series (`./Ionization/Ionization*.vti`) via `XMLImageDataReader`
- Rendered the raw `Scalars_` field as a **Volume** with the **Viridis** colormap
- Computed a `TTKPersistenceDiagram`, thresholded out the diagonal and pairs with persistence ≤ 0.1, and fed the surviving pairs as constraints to `TTKTopologicalSimplification`
- Computed the piecewise-linear contour tree (`TTKContourTree`) on the simplified field
- Rendered arcs as tubes (radius 1) and nodes as spheres (radius 2), colored by `CriticalType`: minima=blue, 1-saddles=white, 2-saddles=orange, maxima=red
