Summary of what I did (since TTK's `TensorFieldTopology` filter isn't compiled into this ParaView/TTK build — I confirmed no such module exists in `libttkBase*`):

1. Loaded `Ocean.vti` (101×101 grid, components A/B/C/D) via VTK.
2. Computed the tensor deviator `E=(A-D)/2`, `F=(B+C)/2`, rotation `G=(B-C)/2`, and discriminant `Δ=E²+F²-G²`, which defines the **eigenvector partition**: `Δ≥0` → real distinct eigenvalues (orange), `Δ<0` → complex-conjugate eigenvalues (blue), rasterized at 10 pixels per grid square.
3. Found **degenerate points** (common zeros of E and F) by triangulating each grid cell and solving the local linear system per triangle, classifying each by the sign of the local Jacobian determinant of (E,F): positive → **wedge** (rendered white), negative → **trisector** (rendered pink), radius 1.

Result: 51 wedges, 48 trisectors, saved to `/workspace/Ocean_eigenvector_partition.png`.
