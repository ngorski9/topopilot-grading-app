**Summary of what was done** (`/workspace/persistence_pipeline.py`):

1. Read `QMCPACK.vti` (`Scalars_` field, 68×68×114 grid) with `XMLImageDataReader`.
2. Computed the initial sub-level set (ascending) persistence diagram with `TTKPersistenceDiagram`.
3. Thresholded that diagram to keep only pairs with `Persistence ≥ 0.04` (the simplification constraint set).
4. Ran `TTKTopologicalSimplification` on the raw field using those constraints, removing all critical point pairs less persistent than 0.04.
5. Recomputed the persistence diagram on the simplified field — this is the final, persistence-simplified (ε = 0.04), piecewise-linear sub-level set filtration diagram (305 total pairs: 15 finite min-saddle H0 pairs + 1 essential/global H0 pair, 6 saddle-max H2 pairs, 282 saddle-saddle H1 pairs, filtered out).
6. Extracted only the H0 (`PairType == 0`, min-saddle) and H2 (`PairType == 2`, saddle-max) pairs via two `Threshold` filters merged together — 16 H0 pairs (blue) and 6 H2 pairs (red).
7. Rendered the result with the full simplified diagram shown faintly for context, colored by `PairType`, camera aligned orthogonally to the birth/death plane.
